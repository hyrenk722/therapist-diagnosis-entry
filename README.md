Entry for the make.fun competition "Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis": one line, at most 200 characters: the funniest diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> Diagnosis: low-grade existential dread, self-medicated with productivity apps. Side effect: you are now very organized about avoiding your life.

Why this entry: A real one-line diagnosis in a calm clinical voice ("Diagnosis ... Side effect ..."), with the laugh landing on the last clause: the patient treated his dread with productivity apps and the treatment worked so well he is now professionally organized at avoiding his own life. Every word earns its place in 144 characters, and the productivity-app-as-self-medication angle is fresh — not a familiar chatbot joke.
